Pharmacy & Patient Precautions

Pharmacy hours: Daily 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM

  • Bring previous prescriptions and eye reports for every visit.
  • Avoid rubbing eyes after procedures or injections.
  • Use prescribed eye drops at exact intervals.
  • Wear protective sunglasses in sunlight and dusty conditions.
  • Do not self-medicate steroid drops without doctor advice.
  • Report redness, pain, watering, or sudden vision blur immediately.
