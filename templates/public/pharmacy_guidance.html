Pharmacy & Patient Precautions

Pharmacy hours: Daily 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM

  • Bring previous prescriptions and eye reports to every appointment.
  • Use prescribed eye drops exactly on schedule.
  • Avoid touching or rubbing eyes after procedures.
  • Wear sunglasses outdoors to protect from dust and UV.
  • Do not use over-the-counter steroid drops without advice.
  • Visit immediately if there is severe pain, sudden blur, or heavy redness.
