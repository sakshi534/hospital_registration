Eye Care Services Available

Our hospital provides comprehensive eye-care services across consultations, diagnostics, procedures, and follow-up:

  • General ophthalmology consultations (adult and pediatric)
  • Comprehensive eye check-up and vision testing
  • Refraction and spectacle prescription support
  • Cataract evaluation and surgery counseling
  • Glaucoma screening and long-term monitoring
  • Diabetic retinopathy and retina consultation referrals
  • Dry eye, allergy, redness, and infection management
  • Minor eye procedures and post-procedure follow-up
  • Prescription management and pharmacy coordination
  • Eye camp screening, referral, and awareness programs
  • Digital patient records, visit history, and search lookup
  • Billing and payment records for consultation/procedure workflows
